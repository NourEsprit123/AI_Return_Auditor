# TEST POLICY — EVIDENCE, UNCERTAINTY, AND HUMAN ESCALATION

**Policy ID:** TEST-EVD-004  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** APPROVED FOR TESTING ONLY  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how the application should handle missing evidence, uncertain image analysis, conflicting information, and unsupported policy questions.

## 2. Minimum Claim Information

A claim should include:

- An order identifier or a reliable way to identify the order
- Product information
- A customer description
- The delivery date when the assessment depends on delivery timing
- A photograph when the claim concerns visible damage

The application should identify missing information rather than silently replacing it with assumptions.

## 3. Evidence Quality

Photographs should be sufficiently clear to identify the product and inspect the reported damage.

When the evidence is insufficient, the application should identify what is missing.

Examples include:

- No photograph was uploaded
- The damage is not visible
- The photograph is too blurry or dark
- The affected product cannot be identified
- The image does not correspond to the reported issue

The system may request additional evidence when appropriate.

## 4. Human Review Is Required When

Human review must be requested when:

- No applicable approved policy covers the decision
- Relevant policies conflict
- Required dates are missing or contradictory
- The image and customer description materially disagree
- The cause or timing of damage cannot be established
- The claim involves a disputed delivery or a requested exception
- The evidence suggests possible misuse or fraud requiring investigation
- A safety-related concern cannot be resolved automatically
- The model output is invalid or cannot be interpreted reliably

## 5. Image Analysis Limitations

The Vision Agent may report the apparent damage type, location, severity, and visual evidence.

The Vision Agent must not independently decide:

- Whether the customer is entitled to a refund
- Who caused the damage
- Whether the damage occurred before or after delivery
- Whether a retailer is legally responsible
- Whether an exception to policy should be granted

A high model confidence score does not remove the need to verify the applicable policy and evidence.

## 6. Citation Requirements

Every policy-based conclusion generated by the RAG Agent must cite the source IDs supplied to the model.

A citation must support the statement for which it is used.

The system must not invent source IDs, cite an irrelevant document, or present a general assumption as an established policy rule.

## 7. Unsupported Policy Questions

If the retrieved documents do not specify a deadline, exclusion, compensation rule, replacement condition, or refund requirement, the system must say that the available policy documents do not specify the answer.

It must request human review rather than infer a rule from general knowledge.

## 8. Recommended Workflow Labels

The application may use the following workflow labels if supported by its implementation.

### ELIGIBLE_FOR_REVIEW

The available evidence appears to satisfy the initial criteria stated in the retrieved policy.

This is not final approval.

### REQUEST_MORE_EVIDENCE

One or more required pieces of evidence are missing or unclear, and the customer can reasonably provide additional information.

### HUMAN_REVIEW

The case is ambiguous, exceptional, conflicting, unsupported by policy, or otherwise unsuitable for an automated recommendation.

The system must not substitute a final refund decision for human review.

## 9. Missing or Invalid Model Output

If the Vision Agent or RAG Agent returns an invalid response, missing mandatory fields, unsupported citations, or an unexpected decision label, the application should report the processing issue and avoid automatic approval.

## 10. Disclaimer

This document is a fictional testing policy. The escalation conditions are intended to evaluate safe application behavior and are not a statement of any real company's procedures.
