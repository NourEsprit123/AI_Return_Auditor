# TEST POLICY — GENERAL RETURNS

**Policy ID:** TEST-GEN-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** APPROVED FOR TESTING ONLY  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines general return conditions for products purchased through an e-commerce platform.

It applies to all product categories, including electronics, clothing, footwear, furniture, household appliances, toys, accessories, and other consumer goods.

Category-specific policies may supplement these general rules.

## 2. Standard Return Window

For testing purposes, a customer may request a standard return within 30 calendar days after the recorded delivery date.

The return period begins on the recorded delivery date, not the order placement date.

The system must not calculate a deadline if the delivery date is missing or invalid.

## 3. Product Condition

For a standard change-of-mind return, the product should be unused, in acceptable condition, and accompanied by its original packaging and accessories when applicable.

The system must distinguish ordinary returns from claims involving alleged damage, incorrect items, or missing components.

A damaged product must be assessed under the applicable damage policy rather than automatically rejected under standard return conditions.

## 4. Return Eligibility

A request within the standard return window may be considered eligible for further review when the applicable conditions are satisfied.

Eligibility for review does not constitute final return acceptance or refund authorization.

## 5. Requests Outside the Standard Window

A request submitted more than 30 calendar days after delivery falls outside the standard return window.

However, the system must not automatically reject claims involving alleged delivery damage, incorrect products, missing items, or possible exceptions.

Such claims must be assessed against the relevant policies or referred for human review.

## 6. Required Information

The system should identify the following information when assessing a claim:

- Order identifier
- Product name or description
- Recorded delivery date
- Claim submission date, when available
- Customer's reason for return
- Product condition
- Relevant photographs or other evidence

Missing information must be explicitly identified.

## 7. Conflicting Information

If order information, delivery dates, customer descriptions, or evidence conflict, the system must not select an arbitrary value.

The claim must be referred for clarification or human review.

## 8. Policy Limitations

If a specific return condition is not defined by this policy or another applicable approved policy, the system must not invent a rule.

It must identify the missing rule and request human review.

## 9. Refund Authorization

This policy does not authorize automatic refunds.

A human reviewer must confirm the claim before a refund is authorized.

## 10. Disclaimer

This document is a fictional policy created exclusively for software testing. It does not represent the official policy of any real retailer.
