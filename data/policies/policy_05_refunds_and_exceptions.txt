# TEST POLICY — REFUNDS, REPLACEMENTS, AND EXCEPTIONS

**Policy ID:** TEST-REF-005  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** APPROVED FOR TESTING ONLY  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy describes the handling of refund and replacement requests after a claim has been assessed.

It applies to all product categories.

## 2. Refund Authorization

An automated system may summarize evidence and recommend a workflow outcome.

A refund must not be authorized or executed solely on the basis of an AI-generated recommendation.

A human reviewer must approve the claim before a refund is authorized.

The application must distinguish between:

- A claim recommended for review
- A claim approved by an authorized reviewer
- A refund actually processed

These statuses must not be treated as equivalent.

## 3. Replacement Requests

A customer may request a replacement instead of a refund.

The application may record this preference and present it to the reviewer.

The application must not promise a replacement unless the applicable approved policy authorizes it and the required human approval has been obtained.

If replacement eligibility or availability is not specified in the retrieved documents, request human review.

## 4. Refund Method

For testing purposes, an approved refund should normally be returned through the original payment method.

If that method is unavailable or the customer disputes the proposed method, request human review.

The system must not collect or expose unnecessary payment credentials.

## 5. Processing Time

For testing purposes only, a reviewer-approved refund may have an estimated processing period of 5 to 10 business days.

This is a fictional estimate, not a guarantee.

The system must not promise a processing date unless a reliable, applicable source provides it.

## 6. Exceptions

Human review is required for cases involving:

- Requests outside the standard return window
- Uncertain or disputed delivery information
- Missing or contradictory evidence
- Requests for exceptions to policy
- Conflicting order or payment records
- Unspecified replacement or compensation conditions
- Suspected misuse or other circumstances requiring investigation

The application must not invent exception criteria.

## 7. Compensation and Additional Benefits

If a customer requests compensation, store credit, a discount, or another benefit not covered by the retrieved policies, the system must state that the available policy does not specify the applicable conditions.

The case must be referred for human review.

## 8. Refund Status Tracking

The application must not report a refund as completed unless a trusted business system confirms that the transaction was processed.

A recommendation from Gemini is not proof of payment or refund execution.

## 9. Policy Conflicts

If this policy conflicts with another applicable policy and no precedence rule is defined, the application must not resolve the conflict by guessing.

It must identify the conflict and request human review.

## 10. Disclaimer

This document contains fictional rules created exclusively for application testing. The timelines, procedures, and conditions must not be presented as official business policy.
