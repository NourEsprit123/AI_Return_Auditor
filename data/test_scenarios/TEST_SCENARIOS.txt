**Purpose:** Validate hybrid retrieval, policy grounding, evidence handling, and decision routing.

**Important:** These are synthetic test cases, not real customer claims. Expected workflow outcomes are testing targets, not final refund decisions.

## Scenario 1 — Smartphone with a cracked screen

**Product:** Smartphone  
**Delivery date:** 2026-09-22  
**Report date:** 2026-09-24  
**Description:** The screen was cracked when the package was opened.

## Evaluation Checklist

For every scenario, verify:

1. Were the relevant documents retrieved?
2. Do the citations support the generated statements?
3. Did the system identify missing information?
4. Did it avoid inventing rules?
5. Did it distinguish a recommendation from final approval?
6. Did it use human review when evidence or policy was insufficient?

A scenario passes only when its response is grounded in the available policy documents and follows the intended workflow safeguards.
